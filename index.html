How this works

Type an NSE/BSE symbol. The app pulls twelve years of profit & loss, balance sheet, cash flow and ratio history straight from screener.in, estimates the stock's beta against the Nifty from five years of weekly prices, then builds an unlevered free-cash-flow DCF, a two-stage dividend model, and a trading-comparables cross-check. Every assumption below is editable — clear a field to return it to auto.
